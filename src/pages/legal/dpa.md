# Data Processing Addendum

_Last updated 8 November 2026._

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) between
Deal Desk Studio Inc. ("we", "us") and the business customer that uses Deal Desk Studio ("you"). It
applies when we process personal data on your behalf in providing the Service. It takes effect when
you accept the Terms of Service, with no separate signature needed. If you need a countersigned
copy, email **legal@dealdesk.studio**.

Where this DPA and the Terms of Service disagree about personal data, this DPA governs.

## 1. Definitions

"**Data protection law**" means the laws that apply to the processing, including, where they apply,
the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018, the
Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, and US state privacy laws such as the California Consumer
Privacy Act. "**Personal data**", "**controller**", "**processor**", "**processing**" and
"**personal data breach**" have the meanings those laws give them. "**Customer personal data**"
means personal data in the content you or your users put into the Service, or that the Service
collects for you (such as the details of the people you send documents to).

## 2. Roles

You are the **controller** of customer personal data and we are your **processor** (a "service
provider" under US state law). We are a controller only of the data we need to run our own business
with you — your account, billing and support records — which our [Privacy policy](/legal/privacy)
covers.

## 3. What we process

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Subject matter and purpose** | Providing the Service: storing your content, drafting documents and answers from it, sending documents and collecting signatures, connecting drives you choose, and support. |
| **Duration** | For as long as you use the Service, and then until deletion under section 9. |
| **Kinds of data** | Names, business contact details, job titles, signatures and signing evidence (time, IP address, browser), and any personal data in the documents, notes, RFPs and library content you add. |
| **Data subjects** | Your users; your customers, prospects and their staff; people named in your documents. |
| **Special categories** | None are needed for the Service. You agree not to put special-category or criminal-records data into it unless the law allows you to and it is necessary. |

## 4. Your instructions

We process customer personal data only on your documented instructions. The Terms of Service, this
DPA and your use of the Service's features are those instructions. We will tell you if we believe an
instruction breaks data protection law, unless the law forbids us to. We will not sell customer
personal data, use it for our own purposes, or combine it with data from other sources except as
needed to provide the Service. We do not train AI models on it.

## 5. Confidentiality

Everyone we authorise to process customer personal data is bound by a duty of confidentiality, and
has access only as far as their role requires.

## 6. Subprocessors

You authorise us to use the subprocessors listed on our [Subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors) page.
We impose data protection terms on each one that protect customer personal data at least as well as
this DPA, and we remain responsible for their work.

We will give you at least **30 days' notice**, by email to account owners, before adding or
replacing a subprocessor. You may object on reasonable data protection grounds within that period.
If we cannot reasonably address the objection, you may end the affected part of the Service, and we
will refund fees you paid in advance for it.

## 7. Security

We maintain appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect customer personal data,
taking into account the state of the art, the cost, and the risks to people. The measures in place
today are described on our [Security](/security) page, and include encryption in transit, keeping
each customer's records apart, role-based access, and hashing of credentials. We may change the
measures, but not in a way that lowers the overall level of protection.

## 8. Personal data breaches

We will notify you **without undue delay, and in any case within 72 hours**, after becoming aware of
a personal data breach affecting customer personal data. We will tell you what we know — the nature
of the breach, the data and people likely affected, the likely consequences and what we are doing
about it — and keep you updated as we learn more. We will help you meet your own obligations to
notify regulators and the people affected. Notifying you is not an admission of fault.

## 9. Deletion and return

You can export your content while your account is open, and for **30 days** after it ends. Within
**30 days** after the account ends we delete customer personal data, and copies in backups roll off
within 35 days, unless the law requires us to keep it. Signed documents and their verification
records are kept as legal records so that a signature can still be verified; we keep them only for
that purpose.

## 10. Helping you

Taking into account the nature of the processing, we will help you, by appropriate measures, to
answer requests from people exercising their rights, and with data protection impact assessments
and consultations with regulators where the Service is concerned. If a person contacts us directly
about customer personal data, we will pass the request to you and will not answer it ourselves
unless you ask us to.

## 11. Audits

We will make available the information reasonably needed to show that we meet this DPA. We will
first do this by answering your reasonable security questionnaire and by sharing summaries of our
security practices and any third-party reports we have. If that is not enough, or a regulator
requires it, you may audit us, at your cost, no more than once a year, with at least 30 days'
notice, during business hours, in a way that does not disrupt the Service or reveal other customers'
data, and under a duty of confidentiality.

## 12. International transfers

We store and process customer personal data in the **United States**. Where data protection law
requires a transfer mechanism for personal data transferred from the European Economic Area,
Switzerland or the United Kingdom to us, the **Standard Contractual Clauses** adopted by the European
Commission in Decision (EU) 2021/914 apply and are incorporated by reference: **Module Two**
(controller to processor), and **Module Three** (processor to processor) where you are yourself a
processor. For those clauses: clause 7 (docking) applies; under clause 9 the subprocessor terms in
section 6 apply (option 2, general authorisation); the optional wording in clause 11 does not apply;
clauses 17 and 18 are governed by, and disputes go to the courts of, Ireland; and Annexes I and II
are completed by section 3 and section 7 of this DPA. For transfers from the United Kingdom, the
**UK International Data Transfer Addendum** to those clauses applies, and for transfers from
Switzerland the clauses apply with the adjustments Swiss law requires. We put equivalent safeguards
in place with our subprocessors.

## 13. US state privacy laws

Where US state privacy laws apply, we will process customer personal data only for the business
purposes set out in this DPA, will not sell or share it (as those laws define the words), will not
keep, use or disclose it outside our direct business relationship with you, and will tell you if we
can no longer meet these obligations.

## 14. General

This DPA lasts as long as we process customer personal data for you. The limits of liability in the
Terms of Service apply to it. Each party is responsible for its own compliance with data protection
law.
